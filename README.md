## My Workplace Operations Approach

I approach workplace operations with a focus on organization, communication, consistency, employee experience, and continuous improvement.

My general process is:

1. Understand the need, issue, or business objective.
2. Identify the people, systems, vendors, and teams involved.
3. Determine priority, risk, deadlines, and dependencies.
4. Create a clear plan and assign ownership.
5. Coordinate execution across stakeholders.
6. Track progress and communicate useful updates.
7. Confirm the work is complete.
8. Document results and outstanding items.
9. Review what worked and identify opportunities to improve the process.
