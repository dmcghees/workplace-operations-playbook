# Workplace Operations Playbook

## Overview

This project is a practical workplace operations guide covering the systems, routines, and documentation that help an office run smoothly.

It is designed to demonstrate how I approach day to day workplace operations, employee support, vendor coordination, facilities needs, inventory, onboarding, events, and process documentation.

## Topics Covered

This playbook includes guides for:

* Office opening and closing procedures
* Vendor and facilities coordination
* New hire onboarding support
* Office inventory and supply management
* Meeting and event setup
* Workplace issue escalation
* Standard operating procedure documentation

## Workplace Operations Approach

My approach to workplace operations focuses on consistency, communication, organization, and employee experience.

A typical process includes:

1. Identify the need or issue.
2. Determine who is affected.
3. Confirm priorities and deadlines.
4. Coordinate with the appropriate internal team or vendor.
5. Track progress and follow up.
6. Communicate updates to affected employees.
7. Confirm completion.
8. Document the process or resolution when useful.
