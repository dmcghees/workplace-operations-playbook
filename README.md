# Workplace Operations Playbook

## Overview

This project is a practical workplace operations guide covering the systems, routines, and documentation that help an office run smoothly.

It demonstrates how I approach workplace operations, employee support, vendor coordination, facilities needs, onboarding, inventory, events, process documentation, and issue escalation.

## Workplace Operations Guides

### [Office Opening and Closing Procedures](office-opening-closing.md)

A practical checklist for preparing an office for the workday, reviewing shared spaces, identifying facilities issues, and securing the workplace at closing.

### [Vendor and Facilities Coordination](vendor-facilities-coordination.md)

A process for managing facilities requests, coordinating vendors, tracking work orders, communicating updates, and confirming resolution.

### [New Hire Onboarding Support](new-hire-onboarding-support.md)

A guide for coordinating workspace preparation, equipment, building access, workplace information, and first day support.

### [Office Inventory and Supply Management](office-inventory-supply-management.md)

A structured approach to inventory tracking, reorder points, purchasing, delivery verification, organization, and budget awareness.

### [Meeting and Event Setup](meeting-event-setup.md)

A practical framework for coordinating meeting spaces, technology, catering, vendors, guests, setup, and post event cleanup.

### [Standard Operating Procedure Creation](sop-creation.md)

A guide for turning recurring work into clear, repeatable procedures through process discovery, documentation, testing, version control, and ongoing review.

### [Workplace Issue Escalation](workplace-issue-escalation.md)

A framework for assessing workplace issues, determining priority, identifying the appropriate owner, documenting business impact, and tracking issues through resolution.

## My Workplace Operations Approach

I approach workplace operations with a focus on consistency, communication, organization, and employee experience.

My general process is:

1. Identify the need or issue.
2. Understand who or what is affected.
3. Determine priority and deadlines.
4. Coordinate with the appropriate internal team or vendor.
5. Track progress and follow up.
6. Communicate useful updates.
7. Confirm completion.
8. Document the process or resolution.
9. Look for opportunities to improve the process for the future.
