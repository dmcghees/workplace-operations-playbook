# Workplace Operations Dashboard

## Purpose

This dashboard provides a high level view of workplace operations across facilities, onboarding, vendors, inventory, events, budgets, and major projects.

The goal is to identify priorities, risks, upcoming deadlines, and issues requiring attention without reviewing multiple trackers separately.

## Status Definitions

### On Track

Work is progressing as expected and no significant intervention is required.

### Attention Needed

An issue requires follow up but is not currently creating major operational disruption.

### At Risk

A deadline, dependency, budget, or service issue may affect operations if it is not resolved.

### Critical

Immediate action is required because of safety, security, compliance, or major business impact.

## Key Takeaway

A workplace operations dashboard turns separate operational activities into one clear view of the function.

It helps Workplace Operations identify problems earlier, prioritize work, communicate with leadership, and make decisions based on overall business impact rather than individual requests.
