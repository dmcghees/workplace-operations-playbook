# Workplace Inventory Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a practical way to monitor workplace supplies, equipment, kitchen items, and other frequently used resources.

The goal is to maintain appropriate stock levels, prevent shortages, reduce unnecessary purchasing, and create a consistent ordering process.

## Periodic Review

Periodically review:

- [ ] Reorder points
- [ ] Usage patterns
- [ ] Vendor pricing
- [ ] Storage capacity
- [ ] Product quality
- [ ] Budget performance
- [ ] Employee needs
- [ ] Obsolete items

Adjust inventory levels based on actual usage rather than keeping the same quantities indefinitely.

## Example

**Issue:** A frequently used conference room adapter repeatedly went out of stock.

**Review:** Inventory checks showed that the reorder point did not account for actual usage.

**Action taken:** Increased the reorder point, created a designated storage location, and added the item to the regular inventory review.

**Result:** Replacement adapters could be ordered before inventory was fully depleted.

## Key Takeaway

Good inventory management requires visibility into what is available, what is being used, what needs to be reordered, and what the organization is spending.

A consistent tracker makes purchasing more predictable and helps prevent both shortages and unnecessary stock.
