### Employment Eligibility Verification

- [ ] Confirm Form I 9 instructions have been provided through the approved onboarding process
- [ ] Confirm employee completes Section 1 no later than the first day of employment
- [ ] Confirm employer or authorized representative is assigned to complete Section 2
- [ ] Confirm Section 2 is completed within the required timeframe
- [ ] Confirm completion status is recorded in the approved HR or onboarding system
- [ ] Escalate incomplete verification to Human Resources or the authorized I 9 administrator
- [ ] Follow the organization's E Verify procedure when applicable

#### Form I 9 Procedure

The employee completes Section 1 of Form I 9 after accepting the job offer and no later than the first day of employment.

The employer or an authorized representative then completes Section 2 by reviewing acceptable documentation and completing the employer verification portion of the form.

Employees must be allowed to choose which acceptable documents they present. An employer should not require a specific document, such as a passport or Social Security card. The employee may present an acceptable List A document or an acceptable combination of List B and List C documents.

Only employees who are authorized by the organization to perform Form I 9 verification should review documents and complete the employer portion of the process.

Form I 9 documentation should be handled and stored according to the organization's Human Resources, privacy, retention, and security procedures.

#### I 9 Exception Tracking

If the process cannot be completed on schedule:

1. Document the outstanding requirement in the approved onboarding system.
2. Notify Human Resources or the designated I 9 administrator immediately.
3. Do not independently change verification requirements or accept unapproved documentation.
4. Follow instructions provided by the authorized compliance owner.
5. Confirm completion before closing the onboarding task.
