# Facilities Request Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a simple way to track workplace facilities issues from initial report through final resolution.

The goal is to maintain visibility into open requests, ownership, priority, vendor activity, employee impact, and follow up.

## Priority Definitions

### Urgent

Issues involving immediate safety, security, property damage, or major operational disruption.

Examples:

* Water leak
* Electrical hazard
* Loss of building access
* Significant security issue
* Life safety concern

### High

Issues causing significant disruption but not presenting an immediate emergency.

Examples:

* Multiple employees affected
* Major conference room unavailable
* Large workplace area without heating or cooling
* Critical equipment unavailable

### Standard

Routine facilities issues that should be resolved but are not significantly disrupting operations.

Examples:

* Furniture repair
* Minor lighting problem
* Cosmetic damage
* Noncritical maintenance request

## Recurring Issue Review

If this issue has happened before, consider:

- [ ] Reviewing previous requests
- [ ] Identifying a recurring cause
- [ ] Escalating vendor performance concerns
- [ ] Updating preventive maintenance
- [ ] Updating an SOP or checklist
- [ ] Considering replacement rather than repeated repair

## Example

**Request:** FAC 014

**Issue:** Conference room video display stopped connecting to employee laptops.

**Priority:** High

**Business impact:** Room was scheduled for several meetings that day.

**Action taken:** Tested multiple cables and laptops, confirmed the issue was isolated to the room equipment, contacted the AV vendor, and temporarily redirected meetings to another room.

**Resolution:** Vendor replaced a failed connection component and tested the system successfully.

**Follow up:** Updated the room equipment log and added the component to future preventive checks.
