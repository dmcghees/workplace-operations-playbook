# New Hire Onboarding Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a centralized way to track multiple new employees through the workplace onboarding process.

The goal is to maintain visibility across workspace preparation, technology, building access, compliance dependencies, orientation, and unresolved issues before each employee begins work.

## Status Definitions

### Ready

All required onboarding tasks are complete or confirmed for the employee's start date.

### In Progress

Tasks are actively being completed and are currently on schedule.

### At Risk

One or more required items may not be completed before the employee starts.

### Blocked

A dependency prevents the task from moving forward.

Examples may include:

* Missing approval
* Equipment unavailable
* Account request incomplete
* Badge request delayed
* Manager information missing

## Escalation Rules

Escalate when:

* A required item will not be ready by the employee's start date
* Building access is unavailable
* Required equipment has not arrived
* Technology setup is blocked
* Compliance requirements remain incomplete
* A manager or owner is not responding
* A temporary workaround is not practical

The escalation should include:

1. Employee start date
2. Outstanding requirement
3. Current owner
4. Business impact
5. Action already taken
6. Recommended next step

## Weekly Onboarding Review

For organizations with frequent hiring, review upcoming starts regularly.

### Review Questions

* Who is starting within the next two weeks?
* Are all workspaces assigned?
* Has equipment been requested?
* Are badges in progress?
* Are there outstanding approvals?
* Are any tasks blocked?
* Are managers prepared?
* Are there recurring onboarding problems?

## Recurring Issue Review

Track patterns that may indicate a process problem.

Examples:

* Laptop requests submitted too late
* Badge requests frequently delayed
* Managers providing information after deadlines
* Missing desk assignments
* Application permissions not ready
* Employees receiving inconsistent arrival instructions

Recurring problems should lead to changes in the onboarding process rather than being treated as unrelated individual incidents.

## Example

**Situation:** Three employees were scheduled to start during the same week.

**Risk:** One laptop was delayed and two building badges were still pending.

**Action taken:** Reviewed the onboarding dashboard, assigned owners to the outstanding tasks, confirmed a temporary laptop was available, and coordinated temporary building access.

**Result:** All three employees were able to begin work on schedule while the remaining permanent items were completed.

## Key Takeaway

Managing several new hires requires visibility across multiple teams and deadlines.

A centralized onboarding tracker makes it easier to identify risks before the employee's first day, assign clear ownership, and make sure outstanding issues do not get lost across separate emails and requests.
