# Vendor Management Log

## Purpose

This template provides a practical way to organize vendor information, monitor service quality, track issues, review costs, and maintain clear escalation contacts.

The goal is to make vendor relationships easier to manage and reduce the risk of missed follow up, unclear ownership, or recurring service problems.

## Example

**Vendor:** Janitorial Services Provider

**Issue:** Two scheduled services were missed within one month.

**Business impact:** Kitchen and shared areas were not serviced as expected, requiring additional internal coordination.

**Action taken:** Logged both missed services, contacted the vendor supervisor, reviewed service expectations, and established a corrective follow up date.

**Result:** The vendor adjusted its scheduling process and service reliability improved during the review period.

## Key Takeaway

A vendor management log creates one place to track relationships, service quality, costs, problems, and follow up.

Good documentation makes it easier to manage vendors based on actual performance rather than memory.
