# Office Move Project Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a practical framework for planning and tracking an office move or major workplace relocation.

The goal is to maintain visibility into project milestones, responsibilities, vendors, budgets, dependencies, employee communication, technology readiness, and unresolved issues from initial planning through post move support.

## Key Takeaway

A successful office move depends on coordination across people, space, technology, vendors, security, budget, and communication.

A centralized project tracker helps make dependencies visible, assigns clear ownership, and gives the project team one place to monitor readiness from early planning through final closeout.
