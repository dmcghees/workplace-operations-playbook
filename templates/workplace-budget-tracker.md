## Purpose

This template provides a practical way to track workplace spending, compare actual expenses with budgeted amounts, monitor recurring costs, and identify financial variances.

The goal is to maintain clear visibility into workplace expenses and support informed purchasing decisions.

## Variance Review

A variance is the difference between the budgeted amount and actual spending.

### Favorable Variance

Actual spending is lower than expected.

### Unfavorable Variance

Actual spending is higher than expected.

When reviewing a significant variance, document:

* What caused the difference
* Whether the expense was expected
* Whether the cost will continue
* Whether another budget category is affected
* Whether approval is required
* Whether future forecasts should be adjusted

## Cost Saving Opportunities

Potential opportunities may include:

* Consolidating routine purchases
* Reducing rush shipping
* Reviewing low use services
* Adjusting food and beverage quantities
* Comparing vendor pricing
* Reviewing recurring subscriptions
* Negotiating vendor rates
* Reducing excess inventory
* Scheduling purchases in advance

Cost savings should be evaluated alongside service quality and operational impact.

## Example

**Situation:** Catering expenses were consistently exceeding the monthly forecast.

**Review:** Compared event attendance, order quantities, vendor pricing, and leftover food.

**Finding:** Orders were being placed using outdated attendance estimates.

**Action taken:** Updated the ordering process to require a final headcount before the catering deadline and adjusted standard quantities.

**Result:** Catering expenses became more predictable and food waste decreased.

## Key Takeaway

A useful workplace budget tracker does more than record purchases.

It provides visibility into current spending, future commitments, recurring expenses, variances, and financial risks so workplace decisions can be made before the budget becomes a problem.
