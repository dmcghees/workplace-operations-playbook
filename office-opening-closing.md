# Office Opening and Closing Procedures

## Purpose

This guide outlines a consistent process for opening and closing a workplace.

The goal is to make sure the office is ready for employees and visitors at the start of the day and properly secured at the end of the day.

## Office Opening Checklist

### 1. Access and Security

* Enter using approved access procedures
* Confirm doors and entry points are secure
* Check for any overnight security or facilities issues
* Review messages or alerts from building management when applicable

### 2. Common Areas

Check shared spaces such as:

* Reception
* Conference rooms
* Kitchens
* Break areas
* Supply areas
* Restrooms

Make sure spaces are clean, accessible, and ready for use.

### 3. Conference Rooms

Confirm that meeting spaces are ready for the day.

Check:

* Displays and monitors
* Video conferencing equipment
* Cables and adapters
* Chairs and tables
* Whiteboards and markers
* Room scheduling systems

Report or troubleshoot issues before scheduled meetings when possible.

### 4. Kitchen and Supplies

Check commonly used items such as:

* Coffee and beverages
* Snacks
* Cups and utensils
* Paper products
* Cleaning supplies

Restock items as needed and note anything that needs to be ordered.

### 5. Deliveries and Mail

Check designated mail and delivery areas.

Sort or route packages according to workplace procedures.

Flag urgent or unidentified deliveries for follow up.

### 6. Daily Workplace Review

Walk through the office and look for issues such as:

* Temperature problems
* Lighting issues
* Spills or safety hazards
* Furniture damage
* Printer problems
* Access issues
* Maintenance needs

Create a facilities request or contact the appropriate vendor when necessary.

## Office Closing Checklist

### 1. Review Shared Spaces

Check conference rooms, kitchens, and common areas.

Make sure:

* Equipment is left in an appropriate state
* Food and waste are handled properly
* Shared spaces are reasonably organized
* No obvious maintenance or safety issues remain

### 2. Deliveries and Sensitive Items

Confirm that packages, documents, keys, badges, and other sensitive materials are stored appropriately.

Do not leave confidential information unsecured.

### 3. Equipment

Follow workplace procedures for equipment that should be powered down or left running.

Do not disconnect or shut down shared infrastructure unless authorized.

### 4. Security

Before leaving:

* Confirm required doors are locked
* Secure restricted spaces
* Follow alarm procedures when applicable
* Report unusual activity or security concerns
* Make sure no visitors remain without authorization

## Issue Documentation

Any significant issue discovered during opening or closing should be documented.

A useful note should include:

* What was found
* Where it occurred
* Who was affected
* Action taken
* Vendor or team contacted
* Current status
* Follow up required

## Example

**Issue:** Conference room display would not connect to employee laptop before a morning meeting.

**Action taken:** Confirmed display power, checked the HDMI connection, tested another cable, and restarted the room display.

**Result:** Connection was restored before the meeting began.

**Follow up:** Replacement cable added to the room supply kit.

## Key Takeaway

Consistent opening and closing procedures help prevent small workplace issues from becoming larger disruptions and create a more reliable experience for employees and visitors.
