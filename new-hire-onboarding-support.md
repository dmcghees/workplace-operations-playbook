# New Hire Onboarding Support

## Purpose

This guide outlines a practical process for supporting new employees as they prepare to join and begin working in an office environment.

The goal is to make sure the employee has the information, access, equipment, and workplace support needed for a smooth first day.

## Before the Start Date

### 1. Confirm Onboarding Details

Verify information such as:

* Employee name
* Job title
* Department
* Manager
* Start date
* Work location
* Remote, hybrid, or onsite schedule

Confirm any special workplace needs with the appropriate team.

### 2. Coordinate Workspace Setup

Prepare the employee's workspace when applicable.

This may include:

* Desk assignment
* Chair
* Monitor
* Keyboard and mouse
* Docking equipment
* Office supplies
* Nameplate or signage

Confirm that the workspace is clean and ready before the employee arrives.

### 3. Coordinate Technology

Work with the appropriate technology team to confirm that required equipment and accounts are prepared.

Examples may include:

* Laptop
* Email account
* Workplace applications
* Video conferencing tools
* Shared drives
* Printer access
* Communication platforms

Workplace operations should coordinate the process without making unauthorized account or permission changes.

### 4. Prepare Building Access

Coordinate required access items such as:

* Employee badge
* Building access
* Office keys
* Parking information
* Visitor procedures
* Security instructions

Verify that access is active before the employee's first day when possible.

### 5. Prepare Welcome Information

Useful information may include:

* Office address
* Arrival instructions
* Reception contact
* Transportation information
* Kitchen and common area information
* Conference room procedures
* Emergency procedures
* Workplace policies

## First Day Support

### 1. Welcome the Employee

Greet the new employee or make sure someone is available to receive them.

Provide an introduction to the workplace and answer basic questions.

### 2. Office Tour

Show the employee important locations such as:

* Restrooms
* Kitchen
* Conference rooms
* Emergency exits
* Supply areas
* Printers
* Mail and package areas

### 3. Confirm Access and Equipment

Make sure the employee can access the workplace and use the equipment provided.

If an issue is found, document it and contact the appropriate team.

### 4. Connect With the Manager

Confirm that the employee knows where to go next and has connected with their manager or onboarding contact.

## After the First Day

Follow up when appropriate to confirm that:

* Equipment is working
* Workplace access is functioning
* Supplies are available
* Facilities issues have been addressed
* Outstanding requests are being tracked

## Onboarding Tracking

A simple onboarding tracker may include:

| Item | Status | Owner | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace prepared | Complete | Workplace Operations | Desk ready |
| Laptop requested | Complete | IT | Delivered |
| Building badge | Complete | Security | Activated |
| Office tour | Complete | Workplace Operations | Completed first day |
| Application access | In Progress | IT | Awaiting one permission |

## Example

**Situation:** A new employee arrived and discovered that their building badge was not active.

**Action taken:** Confirmed the employee's identity, contacted the authorized access administrator, arranged temporary entry according to workplace procedures, and tracked the badge request.

**Result:** Permanent access was activated later that day.

**Follow up:** Confirmed the employee could enter the building successfully before closing the request.

## Key Takeaway

Successful onboarding depends on preparation and coordination across several teams. A consistent process helps new employees feel supported and reduces avoidable first day problems.
